Powers restricted

Powers not enumerated in the Constitution were not granted. Amendment 10 makes this clear: "The powers not delegated to the United States by the Constitution, nor prohibited by it to the States, are reserved to the States respectively, or to the people." Amendment 9 adds that rights not listed are retained by the people.

Infrastructure liberty

Under free markets, in 1902, every US city over 10,000 people had privately funded streetcars. 260,000 miles of railroads were built that operate at 470 ton-mpg.

Violations

The Federal-Aid Highway Acts of 1916, 1944, and 1956 implemented the federal mercantile highway monopoly that mandates burning energy to move two tons to move a person resulting in traffic jams, foreign oil addiction, oil wars, $40 trillion in debt and Climate Change.
